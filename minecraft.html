My minecraft world ;3

Day 1

So I finally bought Minecraft. Everyone's been telling me it's this amazing sandbox game where you can build anything. Started a new world called "Paradise" - seemed fitting. Spawned in a nice little valley with rolling hills and a river. The graphics are... well, they're blocky, but there's something charming about it. Punched my first tree (weird that you can do that with bare hands) and made some basic tools. Built a small dirt hut before nighttime because apparently monsters come out in the dark. Heard some groaning outside but felt safe in my little shelter. This is actually pretty relaxing.

Day 5

Getting the hang of things! Built a proper wooden house with windows and everything. Found some coal and iron in a small cave nearby. The mining is oddly satisfying - just digging block by block, hearing that little *pop* sound when they break. Made myself some iron tools and armor. Feels like I'm making real progress. Started a small farm with wheat and carrots. The animals are cute in their blocky way. Named a cow "Bessie." This game has such a peaceful vibe when the sun's up.

Day 12

Found diamonds! DIAMONDS! Twenty feet underground in this massive cave system. I was so excited I almost fell into lava. Made myself a diamond pickaxe - it's so much faster than iron. Started an ambitious project: building a castle on the hill overlooking my valley. It's going to be HUGE. Three towers, massive walls, the works. Spent eight hours today just placing blocks. Time really flies when you're in the zone. Haven't felt this creative in years.

Day 18

The castle is coming along nicely. Finished the main hall and one tower. But I keep noticing these weird things... like sometimes I'll place a block and swear I hear footsteps. Probably just the game's ambient sounds. Been staying up pretty late working on this project. My sleep schedule is totally messed up, but who cares? This is MY world, MY rules. Found an abandoned mineshaft today - gave me the creeps with all those spider webs everywhere. Grabbed some rails and gold though.

Day 24

Something's not right. I swear there are blocks missing from my castle walls. Blocks I KNOW I placed. Maybe it's creepers exploding nearby? But I haven't seen any damage craters. Started placing torches everywhere to keep monsters away. The nights feel longer now, and I keep hearing that weird ambient cave sound even when I'm above ground. Probably just my imagination. Need to stop playing so late. But just one more wall to finish...

Day 30

WHO IS MOVING MY BLOCKS?! Logged in today and an entire section of wall was just... gone. Not destroyed, not blown up, just gone. Like someone carefully removed each block. But that's impossible - it's a single-player world! Right? RIGHT?! Spent three hours rebuilding it. I'm going to start taking screenshots to document everything. Maybe it's some weird bug. Yeah, that's it. Just a bug.

Day 35

Been playing for almost 12 hours straight. Had to rebuild the same wall AGAIN. Starting to think there's someone else in my world. But the player list shows only me. Always only me. Set up a bunch of redstone cameras around my castle (just pressure plates connected to note blocks) to alert me if anything moves when I'm not looking. The paranoia is getting to me, but I'm not crazy. I'M NOT CRAZY. Something is happening here.

Day 42

THE CAMERAS WENT OFF. Middle of the night (in-game), I heard note blocks chiming from inside my castle. Ran there as fast as I could but found nothing. NOTHING. But the pressure plate was definitely triggered. Footsteps in my halls. In MY HALLS. Started making the walls thicker, adding more torches, more defenses. If someone wants to mess with my world, they're going to have to get through my fortress first.

Day 48

Haven't slept much. Real sleep, I mean. Keep alt-tabbing to check on the world every few minutes. Built walls around my entire valley now. Thick walls, well-lit, no gaps. Nothing gets in without my permission. NOTHING. Found another player structure today - impossible since this is single-player, but there it was. A small house I didn't build. Burned it down. Salted the earth. This is MY world.

Day 55

They're watching me. I can feel it. Always watching. Built a tower so high it reaches the cloud level, 256 blocks up, right at the height limit. From up there I can see everything in my valley. Everything I've built, everything I need to protect. The view is beautiful and terrifying. Sometimes I see movement in the distance - tiny dots that might be animals or might be... something else. Hard to tell from up here. But I see everything. EVERYTHING.

Day 63

Dug a moat around my walls. Filled it with lava. Nothing crosses lava without burning. Basic minecraft logic. Also lined the bottom with obsidian spikes (actually cactus, but they work). Created an elaborate entrance with multiple password doors. If you don't know the redstone sequence, you can't get in. Only I know it. Only ME. Started hearing my name whispered in the cave sounds. That's not part of the game. That's NOT part of the game.

Day 71

Something happened today that I can't explain. Logged in and every single torch in my base was gone. Not broken, not burned out - gone. Like they never existed. But torches don't just disappear! Spent six hours replacing them all. SIX HOURS. My eyes are burning from staring at the screen but I can't stop. If I stop, they win. If I stop, they take everything. This is war now.

Day 78

Built my bedroom in the center of the castle, surrounded by multiple walls and traps. The only way in is through a secret entrance behind a painting. If they want me, they'll have to find me first. But I don't think they're trying to hurt me. They're trying to drive me away. Make me abandon my world, my beautiful world. NEVER. I'll stay here forever if I have to. Been eating nothing but bread and golden apples. Don't need anything else.

Day 85

Ha! Caught them! Set up a trap with tripwire hooks connected to TNT. Someone triggered it and left a crater right in my courtyard. Finally, PROOF that I'm not losing my mind. There really is someone else here. But who? How? I've checked the code, examined the save file, even reinstalled the game. Single-player means single-player, right? Unless... unless they're not playing by the same rules.

Day 92

They left a sign. A SIGN. Right in the middle of my throne room. It just said "Hello." Nothing else. Just "Hello." I screamed so loud my neighbors complained. But how did they get past all my defenses? How did they get to the most secure part of my castle? I've tripled the security since then. Redstone contraptions everywhere. Pressure plates, tripwires, dispensers full of arrows. My castle isn't just a fortress anymore - it's a machine designed to keep everyone out.

Day 99

Can't tell night from day anymore. Been playing continuously, just taking little naps at my desk. If I log out, they win. If I sleep, they take over. The world is only safe while I'm watching it. While I'm guarding it. Found another sign today: "Why are you running?" I'M NOT RUNNING. I'm defending what's mine! Started placing signs of my own: "GET OUT." "MY WORLD." "LEAVE ME ALONE." Maybe they'll get the message.

Day 106

Something's wrong with the world generation. Went exploring beyond my valley for the first time in weeks and found impossible structures. Floating islands that spell out words I don't want to repeat. Cave systems that form faces when viewed from above. Trees growing in perfect spirals that hurt to look at. This isn't random generation - this is a message. They're changing my world, corrupting it, making it wrong. Had to come back to my castle. It's the only safe place left.

Day 113

THE BLOCKS ARE LAUGHING. I can hear them laughing when I place them, when I break them, when I just look at them. Tiny cubic laughs that sound like children but wrong, so wrong. Started building with my eyes closed but that's worse because then I can't see them coming. The shadows move now too. Blocky shadows that shouldn't exist in a world lit by perfect torchlight. Everything is wrong and getting wronger.

Day 120

They spoke to me today. Not through signs, not through sounds, but directly into my brain while I was placing blocks. Asked me why I keep building. Told them because it's MINE. My world, my rules, my blocks. They laughed (everything laughs now) and said I don't own anything. Said I'm just a player in someone else's game. LIES. ALL LIES. I make the rules here. I decide what gets built and what gets destroyed. Started tearing down parts of my castle just to prove I could. Then rebuilt them even stronger.

Day 127

Realized today that I haven't eaten real food in days. There's a pile of moldy sandwiches next to my keyboard that I don't remember making. Outside my window it's... I think it's Tuesday? Maybe Thursday? Time doesn't matter anymore. Only the world matters. Only protecting what's mine. The blocks need me. They whisper my name when no one else is around. They need me to keep them safe from the others, the invaders, the destroyers.

Day 134

THEY'RE INSIDE THE WALLS. Inside MY walls. Found their blocks mixed in with mine - blocks that look the same but feel different, wrong, infected. Started tearing everything down to find them all, but every block I remove reveals ten more infected ones. They're spreading like a virus through my beautiful castle. How long have they been there? How long have I been building with their blocks without knowing? Contaminated. Everything is contaminated.

Day 141

Built a new castle in the sky. Completely floating, no connection to the corrupted ground below. Pure blocks, clean blocks, blocks I mined myself with my own hands. But when I finished the main hall, there was another sign waiting for me: "You can't escape yourself." What does that even mean? I'M NOT TRYING TO ESCAPE. I'm trying to BUILD. To CREATE. To make something beautiful in this hostile world. Why won't they let me just build in peace?

Day 148

The game is glitching. Has to be. Water flows upward sometimes. Gravity works sideways. Pigs fly (literally fly, not just the expression). Creepers that don't explode but just stare at me with those hollow black eyes. The sun rises in the north and sets in the east. Or maybe I'm the one who's glitching. Maybe I've been staring at this screen so long that I can't tell what's real anymore. But the world is real. MY world is real.

Day 155

They showed me the truth today. Pulled back the sky like a curtain and showed me what's really underneath. Cables and wires and infinite rows of servers humming in a darkness that goes on forever. Told me I'm just data, just code, just ones and zeros pretending to be human. LIES. I can feel my hands on the keyboard, feel my eyes burning from the screen light, feel my heart beating faster when I hear those awful sounds in the caves. I'm real. I'M REAL.

Day 162

Starting to wonder if I built any of this at all. Looked at my castle today - really looked at it - and it doesn't feel like something I would make. Too elaborate, too perfect, too symmetrical. Like someone else built it and I just think I remember building it. But that's impossible. I have screenshots, I have memories, I have the calluses on my fingers from clicking the mouse ten million times. This is MY castle. These are MY blocks. This is MY world.

Day 169

Found the sign that broke me. Right in my bedroom, on the wall where I stare every night before passing out at my desk. It said: "You quit 168 days ago." Below that, in different handwriting: "Everything since then has been a dream." And below that, in my own handwriting (when did I write this?): "Wake up. Please wake up."

Day 170

Checked the game logs. Last login: 168 days ago. Session duration: 3 minutes. Just long enough to create a world called "Paradise" and immediately quit. But that's impossible. I've been playing every day. EVERY DAY. Building, mining, defending, creating. My castle, my walls, my torches, my life. All of it real, all of it mine, all of it...

...

But the server records don't lie. Three minutes. That's all I actually played. Three minutes before I quit and walked away and forgot about the game entirely until today when I found these journals on my computer, 170 files I don't remember writing about a game I barely remember buying.

Day 666

Uninstalled Minecraft. Deleted the world files. Deleted myself.

I was never playing a game.

I was playing myself.

And I finally lost.
